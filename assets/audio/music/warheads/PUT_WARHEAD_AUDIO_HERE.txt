Put your warhead audio files in this folder (exact names):

  AlphaWarheadEnhancedAudio.ogg   - plays when the Alpha Warhead is armed
  DeltaWarheadEnhancedAudio.ogg   - plays when the Delta Warhead is armed
  GammaWarheadEnhancedAudio.ogg   - plays when the Gamma Warhead is armed

The countdown is set from each track's length: the warhead detonates 6 seconds
before its track ends. Open the project in Godot once so the files get imported.
(Or put them in %APPDATA%\SCP-Noirverse\music\warheads\ instead.)
